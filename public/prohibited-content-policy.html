Prohibited Content Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Sofia Varelli (sofiavarelli.com) is committed to providing a safe and lawful environment for our creator and fans. The following types of content and conduct are strictly prohibited on our platform.

Prohibited Categories

  • Deepfakes & AI Manipulation: Any content that constitutes a deepfake or uses AI to alter a person’s likeness without explicit documented consent.
  • Underage Content: Any depiction of persons under 18 years of age, including simulated or cartoon depictions.
  • Non-Consensual Activity: Content that depicts rape, sexual assault, drugged, sleeping, or coerced scenarios.
  • Incest: Any portrayal of incest or familial sexual relationships.
  • Watersports: Urination or scatological content.
  • Violence & Snuff: Real or simulated violence, torture, kidnapping, or death (including fantasy snuff).
  • Bestiality: Sexual activity between humans and animals.
  • Illegal Activity: Instructions for building weapons, drug production, hacking, or any other criminal conduct.
  • Hate Speech: Content that promotes hatred, violence, or discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.
  • Prostitution/Escorting: Solicitation of sexual services for payment.
  • Professional Advice: Medical, legal, or financial advice without proper credentials.

Enforcement

Violation of this policy will result in immediate removal of the content, permanent account termination (without refund), and, where required by law, reporting to law enforcement (e.g., NCMEC for underage material).

Reporting Violations

To report prohibited content, please email abuse@sofiavarelli.com. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

User Responsibilities

Fans are prohibited from requesting, soliciting, or attempting to commission any content that violates this policy. Violation will lead to immediate account termination.
