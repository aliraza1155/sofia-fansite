Last Updated: June 2, 2026

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information as long as your account is active. After account deletion or termination, we may retain certain data for legal or compliance purposes (e.g., transaction records for 7 years).

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to enable login and session management. We may also use analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Age Restriction

The Platform is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we become aware that a minor has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.
