Content Removal & Complaints Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Grounds for Removal

Content may be removed from sofiavarelli.com for the following reasons:

  • Violation of our Terms of Service or Prohibited Content Policy.
  • DMCA copyright infringement notices.
  • Court orders or law enforcement requests.
  • Documented lack of consent from the individual depicted.
  • Non-compliance with 18 U.S.C. § 2257 record‑keeping requirements.

How to Submit a Removal Request

If you believe that content on our platform violates your rights or any applicable law, please submit a written complaint to our designated agent:

  • Email: compliance@sofiavarelli.com
  • Mail: [Business Address]

Your complaint must include:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • Specific URL(s) of the content you want removed.
  • A description of why the content violates our policies or the law.
  • If based on lack of consent or age, please provide the name and age of the person depicted (confidential).
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your complaint is accurate.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Review and Resolution Timeline

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 2 business days. We aim to resolve all valid removal requests within 5 business days. If we determine that the content should be removed, it will be taken down immediately. You will be notified of the outcome.

Appeals

If you are the creator or uploader of removed content and believe the removal was in error, you may appeal by replying to the removal notice within 7 days. Appeals will be reviewed within 10 business days.

Reporting to Law Enforcement

We cooperate fully with law enforcement. Any content that appears to involve minors, non-consensual acts, or other criminal activity will be reported to the appropriate authorities (including NCMEC).

Retention of Complaints

All removal requests and associated documentation are retained for a minimum of 3 years for compliance and audit purposes.
